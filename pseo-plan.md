# Programmatic SEO plan — Kitchen Chimney Expert

Current launch set: 60 service/location pages + homepage.

Page pattern:
Brand → Chimney Service → Repair/Cleaning/Installation → Area → City

Each page has:
- Unique URL
- Unique title and meta description
- Canonical URL
- index,follow
- Open Graph/Twitter metadata
- BreadcrumbList + Service + FAQ structured data
- Visible FAQ content
- Local area wording
- Internal links to related service pages
- No fake reviews, ratings, manufacturer authorisation, prices or invented specifications

Important:
- Do not mass-create thin doorway pages just by swapping city names.
- Expand a page only when it adds genuinely useful local/service information.
- Add new pages in controlled batches and monitor Search Console.
